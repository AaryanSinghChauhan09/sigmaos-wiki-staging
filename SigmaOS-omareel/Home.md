# SigmaOS Omareel

Video recording and screen capture for SigmaOS - screen recording and video capture tools.

## Overview

SigmaOS Omareel provides screen recording and video capture tools for SigmaOS, enabling users to record their screen and capture video.

## Features

- **Screen recording**: Record entire screen or regions
- **Window capture**: Capture specific windows
- **Audio recording**: Record system audio
- **Webcam overlay**: Webcam overlay support
- **Format support**: Multiple video formats
- **Compression**: Video compression options
- **Streaming**: Live streaming support
