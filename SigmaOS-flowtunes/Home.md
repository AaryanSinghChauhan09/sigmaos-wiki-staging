# SigmaOS Flowtunes

Music player and audio management for SigmaOS - focused on flow state and productivity.

## Overview

SigmaOS Flowtunes provides a music player designed for flow state and productivity, with features for focus and concentration.

## Features

- **Flow state focus**: Music optimized for productivity and focus
- **Playlist management**: Smart playlist organization
- **Audio visualization**: Real-time audio visualization
- **Focus modes**: Pre-configured focus and relaxation modes
- **Crossfade**: Smooth track transitions
- **Gapless playback**: Seamless album playback
- **Equalizer**: Audio equalization presets
